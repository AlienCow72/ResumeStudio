Crafted Highlights:  

 

In the role of Sr. Software Engineer working in Milwaukee, WI (4 days a week in office), you will be part of the Information Technology team reporting to the Senior Manager, Software Engineering.  This role is a great opportunity for a very experienced technologist. This person is responsible for guiding technology work by collaborating closely with commercial leaders, product managers, and solution architects to understand technical needs and conceive effective solutions within Salesforce and across the technology stack at MCBC.  The role regularly works with solution and enterprise architecture to review and set design standards; they also develop other software engineers within the team. Success in this role is to be the person that is sought out for their ability to solve business problems with technology in practical yet creative way. 

 

What You’ll be Brewing:  

Work closely with product owners and teams to understand their problems and business processes and use design thinking to creatively conceive end-to end solutions that deliver business value and enable development by the delivery team      
Either directly or working with developers, configure Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) applications (e.g., Salesforce) to meet the specific needs of sales and marketing teams. Ensure solutions are minimally customized, use standard approaches, and are optimized for performance and usability                     
Conduct in-depth analysis of requirements to understand desired functionality, then guide engineers in decomposing solutions and identifying dependencies to ensure end-products meet standards and requirements  
Use full-stack programming skills, knowledge, and experience to troubleshoot and remediate complex issues, including software performance and optimization 
Escalate issues and risks in a timely manner to keep leadership and stakeholders informed 
Guide, coach, and review the work of other engineers on the delivery team, applying technical expertise to support successful solution delivery, reinforcing best practices throughout the SDLC (plan, design, build, test, deploy, and maintain). 
Manage projects and processes for a single delivery team, navigating high complexity problems and solutions across multiple domains 
Influence team members through technical leadership and share learnings with the broader organization 
Stay current with the latest trends and best practices in SaaS applications, configurations, and software engineering for the sales and marketing arena

You have a bachelor's degree in computer science or engineering OR equivalent experience

You can apply methods such as design thinking to understand business problems and create simple/elegant solutions, minimizing complexity and customization

You are proactive and curious, with a drive to understand systems and how they elegantly support business processes.

You can collaborate with team members and stakeholders - both technical and non-technical
