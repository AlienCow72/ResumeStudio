IT Cloud Web Application Platform Engineer

WEC Business Services LLC, a subsidiary of WEC Energy Group, is seeking an IT Cloud Web Application Platform Engineer in our Milwaukee, Wisconsin, Pewaukee, Wisconsin, or Green Bay, Wisconsin location. This position currently offers flexibility for a hybrid work arrangement (remote/on-site) with time regularly spent in the office.

This position is part of a job family (Infrastructure Consultant and Senior Infrastructure Consultant) where experience will be the determining factor.

About Us

WEC Energy Group is one of the nation’s largest electric generation and distribution and natural gas delivery holding companies serving 4.7 million customers across the Midwest. We are committed to providing clean, reliable, and affordable energy in an environmentally sustainable manner. Customers are the heart of our business, and we work every day to help grow and support communities where we provide vital energy services.

As a Fortune 500 company, we value and develop our employees who are making a difference in a mission that matters. We don’t just offer a job; we provide fulfilling careers where safety and well-being are paramount. Join our team and experience first-hand our commitment to your success. We offer competitive pay and benefits to recognize your hard work and dedication.

If you’re talented, energetic and ready for a career with a future, we want you on our team. We are powered by a diverse and inclusive workforce fueled by the pride in what we do. If you're driven by the passion to change lives, this is the place for you.

Job Summary

Infrastructure Consultant for the Cloud Platform team is responsible for building and maintaining cloud infrastructure for the corporation. The Cloud Platform Engineer carries both operational and strategic responsibilities. The Cloud Platform Engineer collaborates with development teams to deploy and maintain cloud-based applications and services.

Job Responsibilities

Provision platform as a service and application based services in the cloud

Collaborate with development teams for cloud based application hosting

Develop Infrastructure as Code (IaC) for managing and provisioning cloud based resources

Maintain and govern library for IaC shared modules

Collaborate with on-premise infrastructure teams to align infrastructure standards

Participate in incident response and root-cause analysis for cloud infrastructure

Participate in a 24x7 on-call support role on a rotating basis

Minimum Qualifications

Bachelor's Degree

2+ years of IT infrastructure experience

Preferred Qualifications

Bachelor’s Degree in Computer Science, Management Information Systems or a related field

2+ years of Azure and/or AWS development experience

Azure, AWS, Azure DevOps, Terraform IaC, FinOps, CI/CD Pipelines, or CNAPP experience

Azure Front Door, AWS ALB, Web Hosting, or WAF experience

Production support experience with large enterprise applications
