Crafted Highlights:   

The Senior Manager Product Solution Owner serves as the Product Manager and IT Lead for customer & consumer-facing capabilities, inclusive of ordering, production execution planning, order changes, order fulfillment, digital engagement & notifications, etc. to drive efficient consumer/customer/supplier alignment and high satisfaction with the path to purchase. Working out of our Milwaukee, Wi office, this person’s leadership bridges technical delivery teams, business stakeholders, and voice of the customer to design, enhance, and sustain IT solutions. The position is accountable for optimizing enterprise value by balancing prioritized features, costs, timeline, technical debt, and data considerations.

In the near term, this role acts as the IT lead as part of a broader SAP/ERP transformation program – partnering with a business lead and solution integration (SI) partner leader to deliver a transformed wholesaler ordering process and user experience.  Over time, this role will look after other products such as digital marketing, establishing roadmaps & a clear product vision to deliver against objectives & key results (OKRs).

 

What You’ll Be Brewing:

Lead cross-functional product teams bridging agile (Salesforce.com/o9 Solutions) and waterfall (SAP) delivery methodologies to drive design, development, and delivery of the new B2B customer ordering experience
Deliver and scale consumer/customer technology solutions while informing and supporting the rationalization of legacy tools
Support functional leaders in change management and user adoption of delivered solutions to ensure value is delivered
Own and manage requirements and product backlogs, prioritizing work vs. fixed timelines & budgets; ensure successful end-to-end product delivery including design, development, testing, UAT, and deployment, partnering with the IT department and solution integration partners
